# Portfolio Proof Statement

My portfolio demonstrates my ability to debug full-stack integration issues by understanding their root cause rather than applying fixes without knowing why they work. While building my Lost and Found Portal, I encountered a CORS error that prevented my React frontend from communicating with my Express backend. I discovered that the browser was blocking requests because the frontend (localhost:3000) and backend (localhost:5000) were treated as different origins under the browser's same-origin policy. After understanding this mechanism, I configured the CORS middleware in my Express server to explicitly allow communication between the two applications. This portfolio is designed for a Backend Team Lead at an early-stage startup who evaluates technical reasoning through real projects, with the goal of demonstrating enough evidence to earn a technical interview for a backend developer internship.

## Why

A resume and LinkedIn can list my skills, but only a portfolio can demonstrate my problem-solving process, technical reasoning, and the real code behind my work.
